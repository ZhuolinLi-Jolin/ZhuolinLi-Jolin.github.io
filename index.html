An in-depth analytical exploration

Climate Change Impact on Global Agriculture Production

This project examines the relationship between rising global temperatures and agricultural productivity across different regions from 1990-2023. Through a series of interactive visualizations, we explore patterns, correlations, and potential future implications for food security.

Research Question

How have temperature increases over the past three decades affected crop yields in major agricultural regions, and what patterns emerge when comparing developed versus developing nations?

Key Findings

The analysis reveals a clear negative correlation between temperature increases and crop yields in tropical regions, while some temperate zones have experienced modest productivity gains. Developing nations face disproportionate impacts, with subsistence farming regions showing 23% average yield declines. These findings underscore the urgent need for climate adaptation strategies in agriculture.
